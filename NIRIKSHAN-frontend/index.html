The risk engine prioritizes unusual financial/progress patterns. Investigation view also screens for potentially related works using work description, location, and sanctioned amount similarity.

Risk Rules

  • R1: Expenditure ≥90% and physical progress <60% → +45
  • R2: Expenditure ≥75% and physical progress <70% → +30
  • R3: Timeline exceeded and progress <80% → +30
  • R4: Physical progress <40% → +15
  • R5: Expenditure exceeds sanctioned amount → +25
  • Score capped at 100: Critical ≥70, High ≥45, Medium ≥20, Low <20
